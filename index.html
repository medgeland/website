02 / Published research

Architecture
meets inquiry.

The AI CV Tailor became an applied research case: how can universities scale career support with AI while preserving student authorship, human accountability and institutional control?

AI-Augmented Career Advising in Universities: Digital Ethics and Security Concerns
IEEE · ICCSDFAI 2026 · pp. 221–226
N. Kaiyrgali · M. Serebrennikova · M. Mukushev
DOI 10.1109/ICCSDFAI70505.2026.11647982Read on IEEE Xplore ↗
MethodPRISMA-aligned review of literature from 2018–2026, grounded in career-development theory.
Design principleAI guides, evaluates and surfaces evidence gaps; it does not author the student’s application.
ValidationAn illustrative CareerHub deployment connects the conceptual model to a working reference prototype.
The implementation framework

AI supports the journey.
Humans own the decision.

The paper proposes a five-layer service architecture. Routine support stays scalable, while risk, uncertainty and high-stakes choices move toward accountable human review.

A0
Intake + Integrity GateAI-writing and similarity indicators establish a risk tier and route the case — signals for review, never proof of misconduct.
A
Bounded AI Career CoachGuided questions, navigation and formative feedback on clarity, evidence and structure, without full drafting.
A1
Role-aware Evidence AlignmentSkills and keywords are matched to role signals to surface fit and evidence gaps, not to make hiring decisions.
B
Adviser Co-PilotHuman advisers review, override and finalize complex or high-stakes action plans.
C
Service AnalyticsWait time, escalation, access and outcomes reveal where workflows, content and controls should improve.
Governance cuts across every layerAcceptable use, risk management, transparency, fairness, recourse, audit logs, privacy and data governance are designed into the service—not added afterward.

Personal product · Vibecoded

tend.

A progress companion for days when productivity stops feeling kind.

Open live product ↗

I built tend. for myself after feeling exhausted by overproductivity. I wanted to notice my mood and progress without another streak demanding a perfect daily record. Nothing is mandatory; everything counts.

The product idea

Support should change with the moment. Instead of one generic assistant, tend. has four voices with clear jobs — and it knows when motivation is the wrong answer.

Sad-day modeAll to-dos disappear. The app makes room for a nap and the calming activities saved in a personal SOS kit.
The analystTurns real check-ins into numerical observations and evidence of progress.
The bestieAppears after three completed tasks to celebrate without creating a streak.
The sageOffers calm reflection when pressure or emotion gets heavy.
EspressoDelivers a focused shot of motivation when overthinking blocks action.
